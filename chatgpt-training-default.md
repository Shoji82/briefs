# Does ChatGPT train on your chats? On by default for Free, Plus and Pro; off by default for Business

OpenAI's Data controls article says: "On personal workspaces with ChatGPT Free, Plus, or Pro, data sharing is enabled by default." It also says: "By default, OpenAI does not use content from ChatGPT Business, Enterprise, Edu, or ChatGPT for Healthcare workspaces to train its models." So there is still not one rule for every plan. There are some exceptions to "off", listed below.

Last checked: 2 November 2026. Every OpenAI page below was re-read that day. OpenAI help pages show relative dates ("Updated: 2 days ago"); those are given as shown on 2 November 2026.

Updated 2 November 2026: this note used to say only one page says the Free, Plus and Pro default is on. That is no longer true. OpenAI's [Data controls in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7730893-data-controls-in-chatgpt) says it, and the old article URL (8983130) now shows that same article. The sourcing is rewritten as verbatim quotes, each with its link. Added OpenAI's Trusted Access (Daybreak) exception, the feedback exception, Codex, and the steps to turn training off.

## Short answer

- **Free, Plus, Pro (personal workspaces):** "On personal workspaces with ChatGPT Free, Plus, or Pro, data sharing is enabled by default." ([Data controls in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7730893-data-controls-in-chatgpt))
- **Business, Enterprise, Edu, ChatGPT for Healthcare:** "By default, OpenAI does not use content from ChatGPT Business, Enterprise, Edu, or ChatGPT for Healthcare workspaces to train its models." (same page)
- **API:** "By default, OpenAI also does not use inputs and outputs provided through the API Platform to train its models." (same page)
- **Go:** the default sentence above does not name Go. [GPTs in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8554407-gpts-in-chatgpt) lists Go among consumer plans whose data "may be used for training depending on whether you have opted out".

## How to turn training off

OpenAI's steps for the web, from [Data controls in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7730893-data-controls-in-chatgpt):

1. "Open your account menu."
2. "Select Settings."
3. "Select Data controls."
4. "Select Improve the model for everyone, turn it off, and select Done."

On iOS and Android the page says: open the sidebar, select your profile icon to open Settings, select Data controls, and turn off Improve the model for everyone. "When you're signed in, Improve the model for everyone applies to your account across web and mobile devices."

[5722486](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/5722486-how-your-data-is-used-to-improve-model-performance) adds: "To opt out, turn off Improve the model for everyone under Settings > Data controls in ChatGPT, or select Do not train on my content in our Privacy Portal." and "After you opt out, we won't use your new conversations to improve our models."

## Exceptions to "off"

- **Trusted Access (Daybreak):** "During Daybreak sessions on your personal account, we may use content from your interactions to improve our models even when "Improve the model for everyone" is off." The same section says the setting "still applies to your other activity when not in a Trusted Access session". ([7730893](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7730893-data-controls-in-chatgpt))
- **Feedback:** "For individual plans (Free, Plus, and Pro), if you submit feedback on a response—for example, by selecting thumbs up or thumbs down—we may use the entire conversation associated with that feedback to improve our models, even if you have opted out of model training." ([5722486](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/5722486-how-your-data-is-used-to-improve-model-performance)) For Business, Enterprise and Edu, the same page says "response feedback (submitting thumbs up or thumbs down) does not permit training on the associated conversation."
- **Voice mode:** "Voice mode has a separate control for sharing data for model training." ([7730893](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7730893-data-controls-in-chatgpt))
- **Codex:** "Codex has a separate Include environments setting in Codex settings that controls whether additional context from your Codex environments can be used to train OpenAI models. Changing your ChatGPT setting or opting out through the Privacy Portal does not change that setting." ([7730893](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7730893-data-controls-in-chatgpt))
- **Temporary chats** are the other way round: while they remain temporary they "are not used to improve OpenAI models" and "may be retained for up to 30 days for safety purposes". ([7730893](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7730893-data-controls-in-chatgpt))

## Open conflicts

[What is ChatGPT?](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6783457-what-is-chatgpt) ("Updated: 13 days ago") says: "We don't use content from our business offerings such as ChatGPT Business or ChatGPT Enterprise to train our models." That sentence does not say by default. The Data controls article and [openai.com/enterprise-privacy](https://openai.com/enterprise-privacy/) say by default, with an opt-in. This note does not pick a winner.

[openai.com/policies/how-your-data-is-used-to-improve-model-performance](https://openai.com/policies/how-your-data-is-used-to-improve-model-performance/) (Updated: March 13, 2026) says: "ChatGPT, for instance, improves by further training on the conversations people have with it, unless you opt out". It does not name Free, Plus, or Pro. Its business heading names "ChatGPT Team", not ChatGPT Business. OpenAI's [Business FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8542115-chatgpt-business-general-faq) says: "ChatGPT Team was renamed to ChatGPT Business on August 29, 2025."

The old URL [help.openai.com/en/articles/8983130](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8983130-what-if-i-want-to-keep-my-history-on-but-disable-model-training) now shows the same "Data controls in ChatGPT" article as 7730893, so this note counts them as one page.
