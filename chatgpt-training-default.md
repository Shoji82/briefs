# Is there one ChatGPT training rule for every plan?

Is there one ChatGPT training rule for every plan?
As of 4 October 2026, this note does not state one rule for every plan. Only [https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8983130-what-if-i-want-to-keep-my-history-on-but-disable-model-training](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8983130-what-if-i-want-to-keep-my-history-on-but-disable-model-training) says Free, Plus, and Pro sharing starts on, and the other personal pages do not say that.

## The only page that says the personal default is on

[https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8983130-what-if-i-want-to-keep-my-history-on-but-disable-model-training](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8983130-what-if-i-want-to-keep-my-history-on-but-disable-model-training) (updated last month) says that on a ChatGPT Plus, Pro, or Free plan on a personal workspace, data sharing is enabled by default, and the user can opt out. Once opted out, new conversations will not be used to train. The same page says Business, Enterprise, Edu, and the API are not used for training by default.

## Pages that describe an opt-out and do not say the personal setting starts on

[https://help.openai.com/en/articles/5722486-how-your-data-is-used-to-improve-model-performance](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/5722486-how-your-data-is-used-to-improve-model-performance) (updated 5 days ago): OpenAI may use individual content. The user can opt out by turning off Improve the model for everyone. Business, Enterprise, Edu, and the API are not used by default. Temporary chats are not used while they remain temporary.

[https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8554407-gpts-in-chatgpt](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8554407-gpts-in-chatgpt) (updated 5 days ago): Business, Enterprise, and Edu are not used by default. Consumer plans, including Free, Plus, Go, and Pro, may be used depending on whether the user has opted out. The page is silent on whether sharing starts on.

[https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6783457-what-is-chatgpt](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6783457-what-is-chatgpt) (updated 2 months ago): OpenAI may use individual content, with an opt-out. It says we don't use content from business offerings such as ChatGPT Business or ChatGPT Enterprise, and that sentence does not say by default.

[https://openai.com/policies/how-your-data-is-used-to-improve-model-performance/](https://openai.com/policies/how-your-data-is-used-to-improve-model-performance/) (13 March 2026): ChatGPT improves by training on conversations unless you opt out. It does not name Free, Plus, or Pro. Its business list names ChatGPT Team, not ChatGPT Business.

## Open conflicts

Only [https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8983130-what-if-i-want-to-keep-my-history-on-but-disable-model-training](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8983130-what-if-i-want-to-keep-my-history-on-but-disable-model-training) says Free, Plus, and Pro sharing starts on. The other personal pages do not say that. This note does not pick a winner.

The business pages split.

- [https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8542115-chatgpt-business-general-faq](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8542115-chatgpt-business-general-faq) says OpenAI does not train on workspace data, and that sentence does not say by default.
- [https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8798634-managing-data-sharing-and-privacy-in-chatgpt-business](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8798634-managing-data-sharing-and-privacy-in-chatgpt-business) says both won't train and excluded by default.
- [https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8265430-what-is-a-chatgpt-enterprise-workspace-how-can-i-switch-workspaces](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8265430-what-is-a-chatgpt-enterprise-workspace-how-can-i-switch-workspaces) says default opt-out.
- [https://openai.com/enterprise-privacy/](https://openai.com/enterprise-privacy/) (8 January 2026) says business data is not used by default unless the customer has explicitly opted in.

The 13 March 2026 policy at [https://openai.com/policies/how-your-data-is-used-to-improve-model-performance/](https://openai.com/policies/how-your-data-is-used-to-improve-model-performance/) names ChatGPT Team, not ChatGPT Business. This note reports both names and does not pick one.

This note does not recommend a plan.
