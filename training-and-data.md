# What do the pages say about training and data?

What do the pages say about training and data?
These quotes were read on 3 October 2026. This is not a buying recommendation. One page quoted here is [https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996868-is-my-data-used-for-model-training](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996868-is-my-data-used-for-model-training).

## Anthropic, commercial products

[https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996868-is-my-data-used-for-model-training](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996868-is-my-data-used-for-model-training) Page date 18 August 2026. By default, Anthropic will not use inputs or outputs from commercial products to train models. If you report feedback or bugs, or choose to allow use, they may use chats and coding sessions to train models.

[https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996866-how-long-do-you-store-my-organization-s-data](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996866-how-long-do-you-store-my-organization-s-data) Page date 1 July 2026. API inputs and outputs are automatically deleted on the backend within 30 days, with exceptions the page names: the Files API, a zero data retention agreement, Usage Policy enforcement, and compliance with the law. Saved work chats stay so the product can continue them. You can delete chats from the dashboard. They are removed from history immediately, and deleted from back-end storage within 30 days. Usage Policy flags are kept up to 2 years. Classification scores are kept up to 7 years. Feedback is kept 5 years.

[https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/9796617-can-you-delete-data-that-i-sent-via-team-and-enterprise-plans](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/9796617-can-you-delete-data-that-i-sent-via-team-and-enterprise-plans) Page date 16 March 2026. Team and Enterprise prompts stay so you can see history. You can delete chats from visible history. Backend retention is described in another article. Enterprise can set custom retention timelines.

## Google Workspace

[https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/generative-ai-in-google-workspace-privacy-hub](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/generative-ai-in-google-workspace-privacy-hub) Last updated 14 August 2026. No. User prompts are customer data. Workspace does not use customer data for training models without prior permission or instruction. Gemini accesses customer data to provide personalized responses, such as summarizing a Doc or analyzing a Sheet, under existing controls and the Cloud Data Processing Addendum. Interactions stay within the organization. Gemini does not share data outside the organization without permission. Content is not used for any other customers. None of the content is used for generative AI model training outside the domain without permission.

## OpenAI

[https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6783457-chatgpt-general-faq](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6783457-chatgpt-general-faq) Header says updated 2 months ago. When you use services for individuals such as ChatGPT, OpenAI may use your content to train models. Once you opt out, new conversations will not be used. They don't use content from ChatGPT Business or Enterprise to train models. You can delete your data by following the data deletion process. You cannot delete specific prompts. They review conversations to improve systems and to check policy and safety. Subject to the Content Policy and Terms, you own the output, including the right to reprint, sell, and merchandise, free or paid. ChatGPT is free to use, and subscription plans exist on the pricing page. That sentence does not give plan prices.

The page still prints this trust reply: ChatGPT is not connected to the internet, can produce incorrect answers, and has limited knowledge of the world and events after 2021. That reply is not current product behavior.

## Microsoft

[https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/microsoft-365/microsoft-365-copilot-privacy](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/microsoft-365/microsoft-365-copilot-privacy) Microsoft Copilot accesses content and context through Microsoft Graph. Prompts, responses, and data accessed through Microsoft Graph aren't used to train foundation LLMs, including those used by Microsoft Copilot. Microsoft Copilot only surfaces organizational data to which individual users have at least view permissions.
