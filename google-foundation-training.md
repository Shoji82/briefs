# Are Workspace customer prompts in Google's foundation-model training set?

As of 2 November 2026, this note does not say Workspace customer prompts are, or are not, in that training set. [https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/generative-ai-in-google-workspace-privacy-hub](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/generative-ai-in-google-workspace-privacy-hub) says Google's foundational language models are trained primarily on publicly available, crawlable data from the internet. That answer does not say Workspace prompts are in the set.
Updated 2 November 2026: the Workspace Privacy Hub article now shows "Last updated: October 6, 2026" (this note had 14 August 2026); the site footer separately reads "Last updated 2026-10-26 UTC". It was re-read on 2 November 2026 and the training sentences cited here are unchanged.

Last checked: 2 November 2026. Every page below was re-read that day.

Updated 2 November 2026 (second change): the Gemini Apps Privacy Hub reference below now shows its current date, "Last updated: October 26, 2026" (this note had 24 September 2026). The repeated question line under the heading was removed.

[https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/generative-ai-in-google-workspace-privacy-hub](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/generative-ai-in-google-workspace-privacy-hub) (article last updated 6 October 2026) says Google's foundational language models are trained primarily on publicly available, crawlable data from the internet. Google-Extended lets publishers manage whether their sites help improve Gemini Apps and Vertex AI generative APIs. That answer does not say Workspace prompts are in the set.

[https://deepmind.google/models/model-cards/gemini-3-pro](https://deepmind.google/models/model-cards/gemini-3-pro) (release November 2025, updated May 2026) says pre-training includes publicly available web documents, text, code, images, audio, and video. It also lists downloadable public datasets, crawler data, licensed data, user data and user interactions with the model, other data Google acquires or its workforce generates, and synthetic data. It does not say Workspace prompts are in the set. This list is wider than the Privacy Hub sentence.

[https://ai.google/partnerships-to-improve-our-ai-products/](https://ai.google/partnerships-to-improve-our-ai-products/) says Google trains its AI models primarily on publicly available crawlable web data, such as blog posts and public forums, and that partnerships add closed and offline datasets and paid non-public content. That page is about "our AI models," not labeled foundational language models.

## Open conflicts

The Privacy Hub says primarily public crawlable internet data. The Gemini 3 Pro card lists a wider set, including user data and licensed data. This note does not pick a winner.

Books are named in [https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/gemini/gemini_1_report.pdf](https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/gemini/gemini_1_report.pdf) (Gemini 1.0) and in the December 2023 Gemini Pro card inside [https://ai.google/static/documents/ai-principles-2023-progress-update.pdf](https://ai.google/static/documents/ai-principles-2023-progress-update.pdf). Books are not in the training-data sentences for Gemini 1.5 ([https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/gemini/gemini_v1_5_report.pdf](https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/gemini/gemini_v1_5_report.pdf)), Gemini 2.5 ([https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/gemini/gemini_v2_5_report.pdf](https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/gemini/gemini_v2_5_report.pdf)), or the Gemini 3 Pro card. These are different model scopes. This note does not pick a winner.

[https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961?hl=en](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961?hl=en) (last updated 26 October 2026) is about Gemini Apps chats and activity, not Workspace prompts in the foundation set. This note does not treat those sentences as a Workspace rule. Work or school accounts are pointed back to the Privacy Hub.

This note does not recommend a plan.
