# Google foundation models, and training data

4 October 2026. Maya accepted this research on 4 October 2026. This note does not say Workspace customer prompts are, or are not, in that training set.

https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/generative-ai-in-google-workspace-privacy-hub (article last updated 14 August 2026) says Google's foundational language models are trained primarily on publicly available, crawlable data from the internet. Google-Extended lets publishers manage whether their sites help improve Gemini Apps and Vertex AI generative APIs. That answer does not say Workspace prompts are in the set.

https://deepmind.google/models/model-cards/gemini-3-pro (release November 2025, updated May 2026) says pre-training includes publicly available web documents, text, code, images, audio, and video. It also lists downloadable public datasets, crawler data, licensed data, user data and user interactions with the model, other data Google acquires or its workforce generates, and synthetic data. It does not say Workspace prompts are in the set. This list is wider than the Privacy Hub sentence.

https://ai.google/partnerships-to-improve-our-ai-products/ says Google trains its AI models primarily on publicly available crawlable web data, such as blog posts and public forums, and that partnerships add closed and offline datasets and paid non-public content. That page is about "our AI models," not labeled foundational language models.
