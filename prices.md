# What do the listed AI plans cost?

These prices were read from vendor pages on 3 November 2026. They can change. This is not a buying recommendation. [https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/pricing](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/pricing)

Last checked: 3 November 2026. Every section below was re-read that day.

Updated 3 November 2026: all prices were re-read and none changed. The Claude and Cursor changes noted below were first seen on 1 November 2026.

Updated 2 November 2026: added a Last checked line and removed the repeated question line under the heading. No prices changed.

## ChatGPT

Seen 3 October 2026; re-read 3 November 2026, unchanged.

- Go is $8 a month. [https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/pricing](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/pricing)
- Plus is $20 a month, billed monthly, with no annual billing. [https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6950777-what-is-chatgpt-plus](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6950777-what-is-chatgpt-plus)
- Pro, monthly, is USD $100, $200, and $500. [https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9793128](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9793128)
- Business, per user. Teams of 2 to 200. Standard is $20 a month billed annually, or $25 billed monthly. Premium is $100 billed annually, or $125 billed monthly. [https://openai.com/business/pricing/](https://openai.com/business/pricing/)

## Claude

Seen 1 November 2026. Updated 1 November 2026: Max 5x/20x replaced by Max at $100 and a new Ultra at $200; Enterprise now lists a 20 seat minimum.

Prices on this page exclude tax. [https://claude.com/pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)

- Free is $0.
- Pro is $17 a month on the annual plan ($200 up front), or $20 billed monthly.
- Max is $100 a month.
- Ultra is $200 a month.
- Team. Standard is $20 per seat a month billed annually, or $25 per seat a month billed monthly. Premium is $100 per seat a month billed annually, or $125 per seat a month billed monthly.
- Enterprise is $20 per seat per month, plus API usage, billed annually, with a 20 seat minimum.

## Gemini

US page. [https://gemini.google/subscriptions/](https://gemini.google/subscriptions/)

- Free is $0 a month.
- Ultra is $99.99 a month and $199.99 a month.

## Microsoft 365 Copilot

US page. Paid yearly, per user. [https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/pricing](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/pricing)

- Business Premium with Copilot is $32 per user a month, paid yearly.
- Business Standard with Copilot is $23.50 per user a month, paid yearly.
- Copilot Chat is no additional cost with eligible Microsoft 365 plans.

## GitHub Copilot

[https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/get-started/plans](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/get-started/plans) and [https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/concepts/billing/usage-based-billing-for-individuals](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/concepts/billing/usage-based-billing-for-individuals)

- Pro is $10 USD a month.
- Pro+ is $39.
- Max is $100.
- Business is $19 per granted seat.
- Enterprise is $39 per granted seat.
- 1 AI credit equals $0.01 USD.

## Cursor

Seen 1 November 2026. Updated 1 November 2026: individual plans are now Free, Plus, Premium, Super and Ultra, and Teams seats are priced per plan.

Monthly prices, excluding tax. Third-party model usage is not included in these prices. [https://cursor.com/pricing](https://cursor.com/pricing)

- Free is $0.
- Plus is $8 a month.
- Premium is $30 a month.
- Super is $100 a month.
- Ultra is $200 a month.
- Teams seats are Premium at $30, Super at $100, or Ultra at $200 per user a month. [https://cursor.com/docs/account/teams/pricing](https://cursor.com/docs/account/teams/pricing)
- Teams created before the change keep Standard at $40 and Premium at $120 per user a month.

Notion, Fireflies, Otter, Perplexity, and Canva were left out because the pages didn't support a clean price.
