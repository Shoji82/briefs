# Does a person review Microsoft Copilot prompts?

Does a person review Microsoft Copilot prompts?
As of 4 October 2026, this note does not say a person never sees a prompt. [https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/microsoft-365/microsoft-365-copilot-privacy](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/microsoft-365/microsoft-365-copilot-privacy) says abuse monitoring, which includes human review of content, is available in Azure OpenAI, and Microsoft Copilot services have opted out of it. That sentence does not say prompts, responses, employees, contractors, a default, or a customer control.

[https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/microsoft-365/microsoft-365-copilot-privacy](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/microsoft-365/microsoft-365-copilot-privacy) (canonical [https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/microsoft-365-copilot-privacy](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/microsoft-365-copilot-privacy), page dated 9 July 2026 and updated 1 October 2026 Adelaide) says abuse monitoring, which includes human review of content, is available in Azure OpenAI, and Microsoft Copilot services have opted out of it. That sentence does not say prompts, responses, employees, contractors, a default, or a customer control. The same page says Microsoft 365 Copilot is now named Microsoft Copilot, and that there are no changes to security, compliance, and privacy for organizations. Separate sentences describe content filters and classifiers that analyze inputs and block high-risk prompts. Those sentences do not say a person reviews them. A team reviews AI systems, not customer prompts for abuse monitoring.

[https://www.microsoft.com/licensing/faqs/123](https://www.microsoft.com/licensing/faqs/123) says abuse monitoring terms do not apply to Microsoft 365 Copilot or Microsoft Copilot Chat. It does not say whether a person reviews prompts or responses. A Fabric answer on that page is not about Microsoft 365 Copilot, so this note does not quote it as one.

These pages are silent on the question:

- [https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/microsoft-365/enterprise-data-protection](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/microsoft-365/enterprise-data-protection)
- [https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/microsoft-365/microsoft-365-copilot-architecture-data-protection-auditing](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/microsoft-365/microsoft-365-copilot-architecture-data-protection-auditing)
- [https://www.microsoft.com/licensing/terms/product/PrivacyandSecurityTerms/all](https://www.microsoft.com/licensing/terms/product/PrivacyandSecurityTerms/all)
- [https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/openai/concepts/abuse-monitoring](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/openai/concepts/abuse-monitoring)

This note does not say a person never sees a prompt, and it does not recommend a plan.
