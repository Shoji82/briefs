# GitHub Copilot and training, plan by plan

4 October 2026. Maya accepted this research on 4 October 2026. This note does not state one training rule for every plan.

## What the pages agree on, still not as one rule for every plan

https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/how-tos/manage-your-account/manage-policies says that starting 24 April 2026, Free, Pro, Pro+, and Max may have inputs, outputs, code snippets, and context used to train, with an opt-out. It also says GitHub does not use Business or Enterprise customer data to train, because the Data Protection Agreement prohibits that without customer authorization. The opt-out control is not shown for Business or Enterprise.

https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/reference/ai-models/model-hosting (also https://docs.github.com/en/enterprise-cloud@latest/copilot/reference/ai-models/model-hosting) says GitHub may use individual subscribers' prompts, suggestions, and code snippets to train, with an opt-out, and does not use Business or Enterprise customer data to train. That sentence is printed under Microsoft models and again under Moonshot, not as a page-wide header.

## Open conflicts

Max is named in those two docs sentences. Max is not named in the 24 April 2026 change on these pages:

- https://github.com/features/copilot is a product FAQ, not docs.github.com.
- https://github.com/features/copilot/plans is a product FAQ, not docs.github.com.
- https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/updates-to-github-copilot-interaction-data-usage-policy/ is dated 25 March 2026 and names Free, Pro, and Pro+ only.
- https://github.blog/changelog/2026-03-25-updates-to-our-privacy-statement-and-terms-of-service-how-we-use-your-data/ applies to Free, Pro, and Pro+, and says the list may expand, but does not say Max has been added.

For Business and Enterprise, the product FAQ is a flat no, on https://github.com/features/copilot and https://github.com/features/copilot/plans. Those pages are a product FAQ, not docs.github.com. The docs add "without customer authorization." No checked page says which wording is current. https://github.com/customer-terms/github-generative-ai-services-terms (March 2026) says GitHub will not use Inputs or Outputs to train generative AI models unless given documented instructions, but section 1.A limits that document to volume-licensing purchases and does not name Copilot Business or Enterprise.

The hosting page does not say GitHub's training use of individual subscribers' prompts is separate from a provider's do-not-train promise. This note does not infer that. Provider lines on that page (OpenAI, Amazon Bedrock, Anthropic, Google, Gemini, xAI, Fireworks) stay labeled as provider commitments, and they are silent on which Copilot plan.

This note does not recommend a plan.
