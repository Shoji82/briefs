# Gemini Keep Activity: on by default for personal accounts, 18+

For a personal Google account, Gemini's Keep Activity setting is on by default if you're 18 or over. Google's help page says so: "If you're 18 or over, Keep Activity is on by default." Turning it off still leaves chats saved with your account for up to 72 hours. A work or school account's setting is controlled by the Google Workspace administrator. This note is about Gemini Apps, not Workspace.

Last checked: 2 November 2026. Both Google help pages and the Google blog post below were re-read that day.

Updated 1 November 2026: the Privacy Hub now shows "Last updated: October 26, 2026" (was 24 September 2026). Both Google pages were re-read on 1 November 2026 and the Keep Activity claims are unchanged.

Updated 2 November 2026: rewritten to lead with the answer. Added a short answer, a table by account type, Google's steps to turn the setting off, what off does not stop, and Google's August 2025 rename of the setting. The Privacy Hub still shows "Last updated: October 26, 2026" in its header. This note now adds the Hub's statement that Gemini Live transcripts and uploads are covered by Keep Activity. The default claim is unchanged.

## Short answer

- **Personal account, 18 or over:** on by default. "If you're 18 or over, Keep Activity is on by default." ([Manage & delete your activity in Gemini Apps](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892?hl=en))
- **Work or school account:** "your Gemini Apps Activity settings are controlled by your Google Workspace administrator." (same page)
- **Under 18:** the pages we read do not say.
- **When it's off:** "Even when Keep Activity is off, your conversations will be saved with your account for up to 72 hours to allow Google to provide the service and process any feedback." (same page)

## How to turn it off

Google's steps for a computer, from [13278892](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892?hl=en):

1. "On your computer, go to gemini.google.com."
2. "Click Settings & help", then "Activity".
3. "Near the top, click On", then "Turn off" or "Turn off and delete activity".

The same page says you can also go to myactivity.google.com/product/gemini.

## What off does not stop

- **72 hours:** "Your future chats won't appear in your Activity, and won't be used to train our AI models, unless you choose to send Google feedback. Future chats are still saved for 72 hours so Gemini can respond to you, process your feedback, and protect Google, its users, and the public." ([Privacy Hub](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961?hl=en))
- **Safety review:** "Even if your Keep Activity setting is off or you use temporary chats, Google still uses your chats to respond to you and help protect Google, our users, and the public, including with help from human reviewers." ([Privacy Hub](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961?hl=en))
- **Feedback:** "If Keep Activity is off and you choose to submit feedback, Google collects and uses" your feedback and "context that can help us better understand your feedback, including the last 24 hours of your chats". ([Privacy Hub](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961?hl=en))
- **Gems:** "If Keep Activity is off, any Gems you create will still be saved to your account. Any chats you create with Gems will not be saved." ([13278892](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892?hl=en))
- **Connected Apps:** with Keep Activity off, the help page lists only Device assistance, Gemini Notebook, Messages, Phone and WhatsApp as available. ([13278892](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892?hl=en))

## When it is on

The [Gemini Apps Privacy Hub](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961?hl=en) (updated 26 October 2026): "Google uses your activity to provide, develop, and improve its services (including training generative AI models), as well as to protect Google, its users, and the public with the help of human reviewers." "Your activity is auto-deleted after 18 months. You can change this to 3 or 36 months, or choose not to auto-delete." Auto-delete after 18 months is the retention period, not the starting position.

Gemini Live: "If Keep Activity is on, transcripts of your Live chats and uploads you share with Gemini Live are used to improve Google services, including AI models." The audio setting is separate: "Other activity, like transcripts of your Live chats and uploads you share with Gemini Live, are covered by the Keep Activity setting, which you can turn off anytime." Google's help page says the setting for audio and Gemini Live recordings "is off by default." ([Privacy Hub](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961?hl=en); [13278892](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892?hl=en))

Temporary chats: "Temporary chats are not used to train Google's AI models." ([Privacy Hub](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961?hl=en))

## The rename

Google's blog post of 13 August 2025 said the "Gemini Apps Activity" setting "will be renamed "Keep Activity."" and "If your Gemini Apps Activity setting is currently off, your Keep Activity setting will remain off, and you can turn it on anytime." ([Gemini app personalizes responses based on past chats, plus new privacy controls](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/temporary-chats-privacy-controls/))
