# Gemini Apps, and Keep Activity

4 October 2026. Maya accepted this research on 4 October 2026. This note is about Gemini Apps, not Workspace.

https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961?hl=en (hub updated 24 September 2026): when Keep Activity is on, Google uses that activity to provide, develop, and improve its services, including training generative AI models, and to protect Google, its users, and the public with the help of human reviewers. When it is off, future chats will not appear in Activity and will not be used to train AI models, unless the user sends Google feedback. Temporary chats are not used to train Google's AI models. If Keep Activity is off and the user does not submit feedback, future chats are not used to improve AI models. Temporary chats are not used to improve Google AI with help from human reviewers. The hub does not say whether Keep Activity starts on or off. Auto-delete after 18 months is the retention period under the on heading, not the starting position.

https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892?hl=en (no printed date): for a personal account, if the user is 18 or over, Keep Activity is on by default. A work or school account's Gemini Apps Activity settings are controlled by the Workspace administrator. That sentence is not a claim that Workspace prompts are used to train.

This note does not recommend a plan.
