# Does Claude Pro or Max train on your chats? Yes, if the setting is on

Anthropic trains on chats and coding sessions from Claude Free, Pro, Max and Ultra accounts when the model-training setting is on. Anthropic says new users pick that setting at signup. No Anthropic page we read says whether it starts on or off.

Last checked: 2 November 2026. Every page below was re-read that day.

Updated 1 November 2026: Claude added an Ultra plan. The Claude Code data-usage line now adds Ultra, and the claude.com/pricing training row now names Max and Ultra (replacing Max 5x and Max 20x); both pages and the article above were re-read on 1 November 2026.

Updated 2 November 2026: rewritten to lead with the answer. Added a short answer, a plan-by-plan table, and the steps to change the setting. Added the dates now shown on the Claude Code FAQ and the consumer-terms announcement, and noted that the Claude Code docs now use both Partner Program names. The underlying findings are unchanged.

## Short answer

- **Is it on by default?** No Anthropic page we read says. The announcement says: "If you're a new user, you can pick your setting for model training during the signup process." ([Anthropic, updates to consumer terms](https://www.anthropic.com/news/updates-to-our-consumer-terms))
- **When is it used?** "We will train new models using data from Free, Pro, Max, and Ultra accounts when this setting is on (including when you use Claude Code from these accounts)." ([Claude Code data usage](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/data-usage))
- **What if it's off?** Flagged chats and thumbs up/down feedback are separate cases (see below).
- **Team and Enterprise:** claude.com/pricing lists model training as "None by default". ([Claude pricing](https://claude.com/pricing))

## How to change the setting

Anthropic's steps, from [How do I change my model improvement privacy settings?](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/12109829-how-do-i-change-my-model-improvement-privacy-settings) (3 August 2026):

On a computer or browser:

1. "Select your name from your settings menu and click the drop down button"
2. "Select Settings"
3. "Select Privacy"
4. "Under Help Improve our AI models, click the button to toggle it off/on"

On a phone, the page lists the same steps without the drop-down: select your name, then Settings, then Privacy, then the Help Improve our AI models toggle.

The same page says: "If you decide to turn off the model training setting, we will not use any new chats and coding sessions you have with Claude for future model training. Your data will still be included in model training that has already started and in models that have already been trained, but we will stop using your previously stored chats and coding sessions in future model training runs."

## Used whatever the setting

- Flagged chats: "If our safety classifiers flag your conversations, they may still be used to improve our internal trust and safety models, detect harmful content, enforce our policies, or advance our safety research." ([12109829](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/12109829-how-do-i-change-my-model-improvement-privacy-settings))
- Feedback: "When you provide us feedback via our thumbs up/down button, we will store the entire related conversation, including any content, custom styles or conversation preferences, in our secured back-end for up to 5 years." and "We may use your feedback to analyze the effectiveness of our Services, conduct research, study user behavior, and train our AI models as permitted under applicable laws." ([10023580](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/10023580-is-my-data-used-for-model-training))
- Incognito: "Your Incognito chats are not used to improve Claude, even if you have enabled Model Improvement in your Privacy Settings." ([10023580](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/10023580-is-my-data-used-for-model-training))

## Retention

- "Users who allow data use for model improvement: 5-year retention period to support model development and safety improvements" and "Users who don't allow data use for model improvement: 30-day retention period". ([Claude Code data usage](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/data-usage))
- "If you allow us to use your chats or coding sessions to improve Claude, we may retain your data in a de-identified format for up to 5 years in our model training pipelines. This retention period only applies to new or resumed chats after the model training setting has been enabled." ([10023548](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/10023548-how-long-do-you-store-my-data), 1 July 2026)

## Open conflicts

[Anthropic's privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) (effective 10 September 2026) lists "Inputs and Outputs from our Services (unless users opt out)" as training data. That wording does not name Pro or Max. The consumer articles and Claude Code docs say use if the user allows it, or when the setting is on, and they do not say the setting starts on. This note does not pick a winner.

[claude.com/pricing](https://claude.com/pricing) labels model training "Opt-out" under Free, Pro, Max, and Ultra. The table does not define Opt-out.

The consumer articles [10023580](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/10023580-is-my-data-used-for-model-training), [12109829](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/12109829-how-do-i-change-my-model-improvement-privacy-settings) and [10023548](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/10023548-how-long-do-you-store-my-data) say they are about "Claude Free, Pro, Max" and do not name Ultra. The Claude Code docs and claude.com/pricing do name Ultra.

The [announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/updates-to-our-consumer-terms) was published 28 August 2025. Its page metadata shows a change on 14 October 2026 (UTC). We have no earlier copy, so this note does not say what changed. It still names "Claude Free, Pro, and Max plans".

The Claude Code docs now use both "Developer Partner Program" and "Development Partner Program". [privacy.claude.com 7996885](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996885-how-do-you-use-personal-data-in-model-training) says Development Partner Program. This note reports both names and does not pick one.
